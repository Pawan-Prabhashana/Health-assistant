# Admissions and Registration Procedure

This procedure describes how a patient is registered and admitted at Serendib
General Hospital. Follow the steps in order; reception staff will assist at each
stage.

## Registration

New patients register at the main reception on the ground floor. Bring a
government-issued identity document (National Identity Card or passport). The
registrar creates a medical record and issues a patient identification number in
the form P-XXXXX, which is used for every subsequent visit. Returning patients
only need to provide their existing patient identification number.

## Outpatient Appointments

Outpatient appointments can be booked at reception, by phone, or through the
patient assistant. On the day of the appointment, check in at the department desk
at least fifteen minutes early so vitals can be recorded before the consultation.

## Inpatient Admission

When a clinician decides a patient needs admission, they issue an admission order.
The patient presents the order at the admissions desk, where a bed is assigned and
an admission form is completed. A guardian's contact details are recorded for
patients who are minors or unable to consent. Bring regular medications and a list
of known allergies.

## Discharge

At discharge, the ward issues a discharge summary that lists the diagnosis,
treatment given, medications to continue at home, and any follow-up appointment.
Settle any outstanding account at the billing counter before leaving. Keep the
discharge summary for your next visit.
