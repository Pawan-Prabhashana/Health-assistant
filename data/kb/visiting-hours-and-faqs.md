# Visiting Hours and General FAQs

Serendib General Hospital welcomes visitors within the hours below. These rules
protect patient rest and infection control while keeping families close.

## General Visiting Hours

General ward visiting hours are 11:00 to 13:00 and 16:00 to 19:00 every day. A
maximum of two visitors are allowed at a bedside at one time. Children under 12
must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

## Intensive Care Visiting

The Intensive Care Unit (ICU) has restricted visiting from 12:00 to 12:30 and
17:00 to 17:30. One visitor per patient is permitted, and the nurse in charge may
adjust access based on the patient's condition. Please sanitize your hands before
and after entering the unit.

## Maternity Visiting

The maternity ward allows the partner or a nominated birth companion at any hour.
Other visitors may attend during general visiting hours. To keep newborns safe,
visitors with cold or flu symptoms should not enter the ward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Wi-Fi is available free of charge; connect to the "Serendib-Guest" network and
accept the terms on the sign-in page. A cafeteria on the ground floor is open
from 07:00 to 21:00. Paid parking is available in the basement; the first thirty
minutes are free. Flowers are permitted in general wards but not in the ICU. Lost
property is held at the main reception desk for thirty days.
