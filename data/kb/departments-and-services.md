# Departments and Services

Serendib General Hospital provides inpatient, outpatient, and emergency care
across the departments below. Clinic hours are for outpatient consultations;
emergency care is available at all hours.

## Emergency Department

The Emergency Department operates 24 hours a day, every day, on the ground floor.
Patients are seen in order of clinical urgency rather than arrival, so waiting
times vary. For life-threatening emergencies, call 1990 for the Suwa Seriya
ambulance service.

## Cardiology

The Cardiology department offers consultations, ECG, echocardiography, and stress
testing. Outpatient clinics run Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00. A referral from
a general practitioner is recommended but not required for a first appointment.

## Dermatology

The Dermatology department manages skin, hair, and nail conditions, including
skin cancer screening and minor skin surgery. Clinics run Monday, Wednesday, and
Friday, 09:00 to 15:00. The department performs routine skin inspections as part
of screening and follow-up.

## Orthopedics

The Orthopedics department treats bone and joint conditions, sports injuries, and
fractures. It provides a fracture clinic on Tuesday and Thursday mornings and a
physiotherapy service by appointment.

## Laboratory and Imaging

The laboratory accepts samples from 07:00 to 19:00; most routine results are
available within 24 hours. Imaging services include X-ray, ultrasound, and CT.
Ultrasound and CT require an appointment, which the referring clinic will arrange.
